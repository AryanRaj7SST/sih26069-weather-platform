# India Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) Boundary Dataset

## Source Attribution & Citation
This dataset provides simplified boundary polygons for the Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) of the Republic of India (covering the Indian Mainland, Lakshadweep, and Andaman & Nicobar Islands).

- **Data Source**: Flanders Marine Institute (VLIZ)
- **Product**: Maritime Boundaries Geodatabase: Maritime Boundaries and Exclusive Economic Zones (200NM), version 12 (October 2023).
- **Official URL**: https://www.marineregions.org/
- **Persistent DOI**: https://doi.org/10.14284/632
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY 4.0) (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
